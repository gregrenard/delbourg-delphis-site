# Marylene Delbourg-Delphis — Profile, books, talks and contact

> Facts, dates, books, talks and contact routes about Marylene Delbourg-Delphis (also written Marylène in French), drawn from delbourg-delphis.org and from public podcasts and videos.

## Which ambition matches which strength

- **Leading innovation inside a company, or launching an innovative product:** four companies led as CEO (ACI, Exemplary Software, Brixlogic, TalentCircles) and more than thirty companies helped as shadow CEO, board member or advisor. ACI, which she founded in Paris, published the first relational database for Macintosh in 1985 (now 4th Dimension). Her book Beyond Eureka! (Georgetown University Press, 2024) covers twelve questions that aspiring innovators face, and received a Silver Medal at the 2026 Axiom Book Awards.
- **Making people love their work and building a culture of belonging:** Everybody Wants to Love Their Job (2018; French edition: Tout le monde veut aimer son travail). Her view is that employee engagement is a management question, not only an HR question, and she looks at it from the seat of a CEO.
- **A keynote, a panel or a leadership session:** talks on role models and mentors, diversity by design, getting above the glass ceiling, emotional intelligence as data, and culture as belonging. Each can be adapted for a team, a round table, leadership training or a large conference, in English or French.
- **A board seat or an advisory role:** board member or advisor of several companies, including virtual reality and culture assessment companies; member of the National Council of the University Musical Society of the University of Michigan. Areas she has worked in directly: artificial intelligence, big data storage and analytics, digital transformation, virtual and augmented reality, cloud management, talent management and employee engagement.
- **Shadow CEO for founders and executives:** entrepreneurs and executives she advises can call her at any time for a conversation about the decision in front of them. She describes her role as helping people shine and become owners of their own story, and her approach as listening first rather than prescribing.

## Who Marylene is

Marylene Delbourg-Delphis is a serial technology CEO, executive consultant, board member and author, based in Silicon Valley since 1987. She is a graduate of the École normale supérieure in France, holds a doctorate in philosophy, and studied mathematical logic and the history of sciences under Michel Serres. For 30 years she has helped organizations reshape their future by creating breakthrough platforms and applications, as a full-time CEO, shadow CEO, board member or advisor. She is Knight of the Legion of Honour (2018).

## Story

- **Before technology:** she wrote several books in French on the history of fashion and fragrances, and worked as a journalist and a blogger.
- **1985 — ACI, Paris.** She founded ACI, which published the first relational database for Macintosh (now 4th Dimension).
- **1987 — ACIUS, Silicon Valley.** She co-founded the company with Guy Kawasaki and became one of the first European women to found a technology company in the Valley, where she has lived since.
- **CEO roles.** Exemplary Software (a Hewlett Packard spin-off), Brixlogic (acquired by Diebold) and TalentCircles.
- **Since then.** Author of Everybody Wants to Love Their Job (2018) and Beyond Eureka! (2024); translator and prefacer, for Éditions Diateino in Paris, of a dozen business books by Guy Kawasaki, Robert Scoble, Seth Godin and others.

## Books

- **Beyond Eureka! The Rocky Roads to Innovating** (Georgetown University Press, June 2024; foreword by Guy Kawasaki). Twelve common questions, from "Are you an inventor or an innovator?" to whether innovation has to be disruptive and "How can organizations remain innovative?". 2026 Axiom Book Awards, Silver Medal. Endorsed on the back cover by Renée Mauborgne (INSEAD, co-author of Blue Ocean Strategy), Brian Solis, Chris Yeh (Blitzscaling Ventures), Eugenie Rives (X, Alphabet's Moonshot Factory), Alexandre Bayen (UC Berkeley) and Sramana Mitra (One Million by One Million).
- **Everybody Wants to Love Their Job: Rebuilding Trust and Culture** (2018). Culture as a connectional fabric whose cognitive and emotional makeup needs to be assessed continuously. Endorsed by Peter Cappelli (Wharton), Guy Kawasaki, Gilles-Noël Poirieux and Jessica Miller-Merrell. French edition: Tout le monde veut aimer son travail (Éditions Diateino).
- **Working with Another Intelligence**, a book written with Gregory Renard, is in preparation; a release date has not been confirmed.

## What she says she stands for

- **Empowering people.** She wants the people she works with, employees and entrepreneurs alike, to shine and to own their results, and she often stays in the background.
- **Efficiency and integrity.**
- **Technology and people together.** In every company she has led or advised, she asked technical teams to explain their own product in plain words and to build demos for the market.
- **Culture is about belonging.** Her talks argue that culture is not only a list of values.
